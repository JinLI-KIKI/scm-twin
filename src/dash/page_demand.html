Two Scorecards for One Transfer

A US tariff on China-made laptops starts at week 0 of the fiscal year. The brand passes part of it into the channel price, the price costs units, and the US volume can leave China in any week. The BU scores each choice on its KPIs, the shipment target in sell-in units and MWD; the company scores it on the total cost of the year. They do not always agree.

Option ledger

Demand. Each week the US plan of each model (lifecycle curve of Module 1, 40% US share) meets a price rise: pass-through × tariff × the share of the week's units still made in China, from the repricing week on. Units follow a constant elasticity: q = plan × (1 + rise / channel price)elasticity. The tariff is the rate × customs value, less the share foreign exporters absorb. After the cutover the new site takes over linearly over the ramp weeks; China builds the rest and pays the tariff.

BU view (R33, R1, R8). Shipment attainment = sell-in units against the plan made before the tariff. MWD as the BU's KPI counts it: claimed MWD, every plan cancelled inside the 16-week liability window at full BOM value (Module 2), or physical MWD after netting, relocation and rework; both plus the extra end-of-life MWD of the longer window (Module 3) for the rest of the year. The BU picks the latest option that still reaches the target tolerance, since every week later means less MWD; if none does, the one closest to target. Tariffs are not a BU KPI.

Company view. Tariff the brand absorbs (paid on China-made units minus what the price rise recovers) + gross margin on the units the price rise loses + physical MWD, holding, relocation and rework of the cutover (Module 2) + parts expediting and the longer chain (end-of-life MWD and pipeline carrying, Module 3, for the rest of the year) + the new site's conversion uplift on its units. One fiscal year only: the tariff saved in later years is on the Business case page.

Status. Pass-through, the repricing lag and exporter absorption come from 2025 price moves and the Kiel Institute; elasticity from 2026 US PC data (Omdia, confounded by the memory shortage); the claimed-MWD rule is practitioner input; the target tolerance, ramp and margins are placeholders (data/demand_params.csv, data/demand_models.csv).
