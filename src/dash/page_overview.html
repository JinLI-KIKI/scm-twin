Laptop Supply Chain Twin

An anonymous laptop maker builds in China and is weighing a move to Indonesia because of tariffs. The twin prices what a move really costs (material left without demand, timing, a slow ramp, a longer chain, old stock at the next launch) and follows one fiscal year week by week. It runs on 2026 as it is: the Strait of Hormuz closed, ocean rates several times their pre-war level, no US duty on laptops after the Supreme Court ruling, and memory prices multiplied.
