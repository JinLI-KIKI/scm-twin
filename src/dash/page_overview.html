Supply chain digital twin · laptops built in Asia, sold worldwide

Laptop Supply Chain Twin

An anonymous laptop maker builds in China and is weighing a move to Indonesia because of tariffs. Five regional distribution centres (MGI) serve the US, Europe, the UK, Japan and Brazil. The model prices what a move really costs: material left without demand (MWD), timing, a longer chain, a slow ramp and old stock when the next model launches.
