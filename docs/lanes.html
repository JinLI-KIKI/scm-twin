How it is computed. Ocean distance from the open-source searoute network at 15 knots plus 4 days for intermediate calls; the Cape option closes Suez and Bab-el-Mandeb, which most Asia–Europe services still avoid in 2026. Air legs are great circles; Brazil air goes via Frankfurt. Truck legs follow the Natural Earth road network. Handling days per step are in data/leadtime_params.csv (all placeholders).

Duty. Import duty on laptops by market from data/tariffs.csv: EU, UK and Japan zero under the WTO Information Technology Agreement (to verify); Brazil 16% plus IPI. US laptops are exempt from the 2026 Section 301 action; a Section 232 semiconductor phase 2 could still reach them.
