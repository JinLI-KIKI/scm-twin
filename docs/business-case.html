What moves the answer

Change in transfer-year net, one input at a time

Benefit. Tariff saved = US weekly units × customs value × (China rate − Indonesia rate) × 52.

Recurring costs. Extra end-of-life MWD because plans are committed 20 instead of 16 weeks ahead with forecast accuracy about 8 points worse (R17, R18); carrying cost of 4 more weeks of material in the chain; new-site conversion cost +5–10% (R20). One-time costs. Tariff still paid on volume the new site cannot cover while it ramps (R16); expediting parts while the longer lead time is not yet covered (R21). Ramp length depends on GSM strength.
