Laptop Supply Chain Twin

An anonymous laptop maker builds in China and is weighing a move to Indonesia because of tariffs. The model prices what a move really costs: material left without demand (MWD), timing, a longer chain, a slow ramp and old stock when the next model launches. A wider footprint view adds Vietnam, Malaysia, Brazil and the UAE as candidate ODM sites and nine regional distribution centres (MGIs), priced under the laptop duties, ocean rates and Hormuz closure of October 2026, and checked against where US imports actually moved.
