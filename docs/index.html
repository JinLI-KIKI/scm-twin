What is real here

  • Ocean paths and distances: the open-source searoute maritime network, with Suez and Bab-el-Mandeb closed for the Cape option.
  • Country shapes: Natural Earth, public domain.
  • Duty rates: public sources, linked below, as of September 2026.
  • Factory and MGI locations are illustrative. Every handling and transit day is a first estimate to be calibrated.
