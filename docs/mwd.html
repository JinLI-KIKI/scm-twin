Where Cancelled Plans Leave Material

When plans inside the -week liability window are cancelled, their material is still owed. Some is netted by other plans that use the same part number, some is converted at a cost, and the rest becomes MWD.

Routing classes. 1 = same part number used elsewhere, netted by MRP (CPU, memory, SSD, panel, battery, adapter, housing). 2 = convertible for another region at a cost, after cross-BU agreement (power cord, packaging). 3 = locked to the cancelled configuration (keyboard, WWAN) (R6, R7).

Netting. Freed stock offsets other plans from the end of its non-cancellable window: 2 weeks for industry-standard parts shared across brands at the ODM, the full lead time for custom parts (R3, R10). Scenario C0 uses the older assumption, netting only beyond the purchase lead time. Holding cost accrues at 20% a year while stock waits; batteries need recharging and chips past their date code need re-testing.

Status. Rules from practitioner input; unit costs, lead times and lifecycles are placeholders. See the Rules register.
